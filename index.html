Behavioral Architecture

Turning Apathy into Civic Habit:
The B=MAT Cognitive Framework

During the Orange Grove Incubation Program, our team mapped the psychological reasons why traditional political participation fails young citizens. We replace alienated cynicism with engineered ritual loops.

M

Motivation (Intrinsic & Extrinsic)

Traditional politics offers no immediate agency. Democracy Game introduces peer status, rhetorical competition, and the verifiable knowledge that synthesized debate briefs reach real MEPs and local government decision-makers.

→ Kahneman & Tversky heuristic alignment
A

Ability (Radical Simplification)

Citizens are alienated by 400-page policy directives. Our AI micro-summarizes EU legislation into digestible debate motions, providing instant data cards and pre-verified citations with one click.

→ Frictionless deliberative entry point
T

Trigger (The Deliberation Call)

Instead of toxic push notifications, users receive "Calls to the Floor" when regional votes or legislation directly impacting their age cohort (student debt, housing, green energy) enter deliberation.

→ Habit-forming ritual consumption model
Founding Leadership • Brussels 2018 – Athens 2026

"People, and especially young people, eager to communicate with each other — and communicate the truth."

The concept for Democracy Game was formulated in 2018 in the district of Etterbeek, Brussels, during Dimitrios Lampiris's service at the European Parliament alongside Greek MEP Giorgos Grammatikakis.

Observing the inner mechanisms of European decision-making in contrast with the pervasive disengagement of Greek and European youth revealed an alarming structural failure: formal democratic proceedings had become completely untranslatable to digital natives, while algorithm-driven disinformation was systematically eroding social cohesion.

Developed as a recognized finalist at the EU Datathon 2021 and nurtured through the rigorous curriculum of the Orange Grove Incubation Program, the platform is now revitalized under focused executive leadership, integrating cutting-edge LLMs and real-time open data verification.
